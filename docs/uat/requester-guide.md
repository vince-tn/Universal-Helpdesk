# Service Desk: How to Ask for Help

For all staff and client teams.

## Sign in

- First time? Sign up with your work email. We send you a code. Enter it.
- After that, log in with your password or Sign in with Microsoft.
- Turn on MFA in Account settings. Keep your recovery codes safe.

## Raise a ticket

- Click New ticket.
- Pick the service that fits, for example Password reset or Certificate of employment.
- Not sure? Pick the closest one. We move it if needed.
- Say what happened, when, and what you need.
- Answer the impact questions honestly. They set the priority.
- Add a screenshot, PDF, Word or Excel file if it helps.

## What happens next

- You get an email with your ticket number.
- The assistant replies first. It may suggest a quick fix.
- A person from the right team picks it up.
- The assistant cannot approve requests, promise pay amounts or give dates. The team will.

## Keep it moving

- Reply on the ticket or to the email. Do not open a new ticket for the same issue.
- If we ask a question, the clock waits for your answer.
- When we resolve it, check it works. Not fixed? Just reply and it reopens.

## Private matters

- Payroll, pay disputes and workplace concerns are private.
- Only the right HR people can see them.

## After it is closed

- You get a short survey. One click helps us improve.

# Service Desk: Staff Guide

For agents, Team Leaders and Supervisors.

## Your day

- Log in. Use MFA or Sign in with Microsoft.
- Open your Team queue. Start with Overdue, then High priority.
- Take a ticket by assigning it to yourself.

## Working a ticket

- Read the request and the assistant's first reply.
- Wrong team? Move it. Do not leave it for someone else to find.
- Reply to the requester in plain words. Use a canned reply to save time.
- Need to talk to your team only? Use an internal note. The requester never sees it.
- Waiting on the requester? Set the status to Waiting. The SLA clock stops.

## Resolving

- A ticket needs an assignee and a resolution note before you can resolve it.
- Write the note so the next person can follow it. Good notes become knowledge articles.
- If the requester replies after you resolve, the ticket reopens to you.

## The assistant

- It sorts tickets and writes the first reply. It can be wrong.
- It never approves anything, never gives amounts or dates. Only staff do.
- HR Payroll, Employee Relations and Audit tickets use the local AI only. They stay on our server.
- If a reply is wrong, correct it in your own reply and tell your Team Leader.

## Approvals

- Some requests need a Team Leader, then a Supervisor.
- Approve or reject from the ticket. Always give a reason when you reject.

## Confidential tickets

- Only the right HR or Compliance staff can see them.
- Do not copy their details into other tickets, chats or emails.

## Need help?

Ask your Team Leader, or raise an IT ticket under Service Desk support.
